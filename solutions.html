02 Live · free to use 2026

Decomposer

Paste a song. It comes apart in layers.

A web app that takes a song from a link and shows its style, its minimal layers, the arrangement and how to rebuild the sound with only what ships in Ableton Live.

Open Decomposer ↗

Python · Gemini · Web Audio · WebGL · Three.js · Vercel

  1. PasteA link from YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud or Bandcamp.
  2. Break downThe minimal layers, each in its frequency band.
  3. RebuildStep by step, with only what ships in Ableton Live.
  4. CollectEvery song lands on a shared musical spectrum.

Not AI music. AI to learn how it's made.
